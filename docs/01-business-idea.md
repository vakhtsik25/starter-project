## Part B — Idea definition (fill in after you commit)

### The one-liner
> We help **[who]** to **[do what]** by **[how]**, unlike **[the current alternative]**.

### Problem
What's broken today, and who feels it most acutely?

### Target customer
Be specific. "Small business owners" is too broad. "Solo wedding photographers who bill under $50k/yr" is usable.

### Our solution
What do we actually offer? Describe it as if to a friend.

### Why now / why us
What changed in the world that makes this possible now, and what's our unfair advantage?

### How we make money
One or two sentences. Even a rough guess.

### Riskiest assumption
The one thing that, if false, kills the whole idea. This becomes the first thing the roadmap tests.
